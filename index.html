    Speech Gender Gap

    2023–2026 speeches

    Given the public stage, do women and men country leaders prioritize different issues?

    Speeches by heads of state and government from 2023 to 2026, machine-translated where needed, with NMF topic models fit per leader and compared by sex and by domestic vs. international audience. The master dataset feeds four tracks: UN General Assembly speeches, the 2026 UN Secretary-General candidates, about 10 paired case studies (a female leader and a male comparator), and a pooled corpus of 20+ leaders.

    early signals Most leaders say different things at home than abroad, not just the same themes reworded. Mexico's Sheinbaum is a notable exception: she keeps leading with domestic achievements on the world stage.

    • ~7,800documents from 20+ sources in 15 countries
    • 5%of the data was all one API returned. I found it, fixed it, and checked the fix against ground truth. The bug had reversed a key finding.
    • 0silent duplicates allowed through: Jaccard-similarity dedup and term-anchor tests run before anything reaches the models

    Gender Bias in American Language

    A century of US public text

    Has gender bias in US public language changed over a century, and does it track real inequality?

    Extends Garg et al. (2018) from national-by-decade down to state-by-year, scoring bias with WEAT (Caliskan et al., 2017) on career vs. family, competence vs. warmth, leadership vs. subordination, and occupations. The panel will feed a model of women's labor-force participation.

    Text
    Chronicling America newspapers, GDELT, Reddit, Congressional Record
    Models
    Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT, RoBERTa
    Checks
    Census occupation data, General Social Survey
    Scale
    36M+ records, 5 sources, 9 demo states; checkpointed and fault-tolerant

    preliminary The demo pipeline runs end to end and reproduces the known century-long drop in occupational segregation. Firm results wait on full-scale data.

    Document Q&A API

    2026 · live

    Why I built it

    While studying for my cloud computing midterm, I spent hours going through lecture slides, notes and Google results just to find one answer. So I built a tool to cut that search time. It gives the exact answer from reliable sources only, and if the answer isn't there, it says so instead of making one up.

    A serverless RAG API on AWS. Upload your own documents and ask questions about them. Answers come only from those documents, with citations, and the API declines when it can't find support. If the main LLM provider fails, it falls back to a second one automatically.

    AI Shopping Assistant

    2026 · live

    The vision

    Shopping for an occasion means scrolling through thousands of options. I want an agent that understands the event and puts together the whole look, including the outfit, jewelry and accessories, from real online stores. What's live today is an early, small-scale first version that I plan to grow much larger.

    Ask for "an outdoor wedding outfit under $200" and a LangGraph agent turns it into tool calls over a 44,417-item catalog served by my own MCP server. Write actions need an API key, a human confirmation and pass a rate limit, and every attempt is logged.
